Kotihom introduction

Rashtraguru Yogi Naraharinath had performed a total of 129 Kotihom Mahayagyas in Nepal and abroad for the welfare of the people, national unity and the awakening of Vedic Sanatan Dharma.

Kotihom refers to an extremely grand and sacred religious ritual in which a total of one crore ahutis are offered into the hawan kunda according to Vedic and Tantric methods. This great campaign, which began at Devghat in B.S. 2012, made a major contribution to preserving historic records that were on the verge of disappearing and to spreading cultural awareness.

Conducted with 108 hawan kundas and the participation of hundreds of Vedic-chanting Brahmins, these Kotihoms carried a message of natural-disaster prevention and world peace.

About the Yagya

What is Kotihom?

Koti means one crore (ten million). Kotihom is a Hawan, a sacred fire offering, in which one crore ahutis are offered into the consecrated fire (Agni) with mantras, led by priests. In the Hindu tradition, Agni is regarded as the messenger who carries each offering to the gods.

Why one crore ahutis?

The organizers have resolved to complete one crore (10,000,000) ahutis, praying together for the peace, harmony and lasting prosperity of Nepal and all its people.

        Questions donors often ask

        Is my contribution recorded?

        Yes. When you submit the form, your contribution is saved as pending. An organizer then checks it against the payment and marks it verified. Only then does it appear in the public list. Contributions received offline are recorded by the organizers in the same way.

        When will my contribution show on the page? I paid, but I am not in the list.

        It shows after the organizers have matched your payment. This is done by people, not automatically, so it takes some time. If it has not appeared, please contact the organizers and quote your reference code.

        Can I contribute from abroad?

        You can submit the form from any country and choose your currency (NPR, USD, INR, EUR, GBP or AUD). The ways to send from abroad are shown in the 'From abroad' tab once the organizers have published them. The value in Nepali rupees is fixed when your payment is verified, so the amount listed may differ slightly from the estimate you see.

        Can I contribute anonymously? Who sees my details?

        Yes. Choose 'Anonymous' and your name will not be shown. The public list shows only these details: the name you chose (or none), your country, the amount and currency, the payment method, the date, your message, and the reference code. Your phone, email, receipt and transaction number are never shown publicly. The organizers can still see who you are, for their records.

        Will I get a receipt? How is my contribution treated for tax?

        Please keep your own payment receipt or screenshot.

        Whether an official receipt can be issued, and how a contribution is treated for tax, will be announced here once the organizers have confirmed it. No promise about either is made at this time.

        What is an ahuti?

        An ahuti is a single offering made into the sacred fire during a Hawan, accompanied by a mantra and the word 'Svaha'. This Yagya resolves to complete one crore (10,000,000) such offerings.

        Can I attend in person?

        The date has not been announced yet, and the venue is proposed (see above). When they are confirmed, this page will say whether and how devotees can attend.

        How can I see where the money goes?

        Every verified contribution is listed in the public list, and the organizers publish their expenses in the 'Where the funds go' table, as they record them, with the total spent. Please look at both from time to time, and contact the organizers with any question.
